To store solar energy during the day for use at night, you generally need an **energy storage system**. The most common options are:

### 1. Solar Battery Storage
This is the most practical home solution.

**How it works:**
- Solar panels generate electricity during the day.
- Your home uses some of it immediately.
- Extra electricity charges a battery.
- At night, your home draws power from the battery.

Common battery types:
- **Lithium-ion batteries** — most common, efficient, compact
- **LiFePO₄ batteries** — long-lasting and safer
- **Lead-acid batteries** — cheaper but shorter lifespan

Examples:
- Tesla Powerwall
- Enphase IQ Battery
- LG Chem RESU
- EcoFlow / Bluetti / Jackery systems for smaller setups

---

### 2. Grid-Tied Solar With Net Metering
If your area allows net metering, you may not need a battery.

**How it works:**
- Extra solar power goes to the electric grid during the day.
- You receive credits from the utility.
- At night, you use grid power and apply those credits.

This is often cheaper than batteries, but it does not usually work during power outages unless you have backup equipment.

---

### 3. Hybrid Solar System
A hybrid system combines:
- Solar panels
- Battery storage
- Grid connection

This gives you:
- Daytime solar power
- Nighttime battery power
- Grid backup when needed
- Possible outage protection

---

### 4. Thermal Storage
If your main need is heating water or indoor heating, you can store solar energy as heat.

Examples:
- Solar water heaters
- Hot water storage tanks
- Thermal mass systems
- Heated floor systems

This is useful for hot water and heating, but not for powering lights or appliances.

---

### Basic Setup Needed

For a home solar storage system, you typically need:

1. **Solar panels**
2. **Charge controller or hybrid inverter**
3. **Battery bank**
4. **Inverter**
5. **Electrical safety equipment**
6. **Optional grid connection**

---

### Simple Example

If your solar panels produce extra energy during the day:

- Daytime: panels power your home and charge the battery
- Evening: battery powers lights, fridge, TV, Wi-Fi, etc.
- Late night: if battery is empty, you use grid power or a generator

---

### Best Choice

For most homes, the best solution is:

**Solar panels + lithium battery + hybrid inverter**

This lets you store daytime solar energy and use it at night efficiently.
