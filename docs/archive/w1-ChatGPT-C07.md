## What to look for in a 2026 home battery

For most homeowners, the best battery will be an **LFP — lithium iron phosphate — system** because LFP is generally favored for safety, lifespan, and residential performance [1][4]. A typical home battery setup should be sized around **10–15 kWh for essential backup or time-of-use savings**, while **20–30+ kWh** may be needed for whole-home backup with HVAC and large appliances [4].

Key specs to compare:

1. **Usable capacity:** Usually measured in kWh; this determines how long your battery can run your home [1].
2. **Continuous and peak power:** This determines whether the battery can start and run large appliances like AC units, pumps, refrigerators, and EV chargers [4].
3. **Round-trip efficiency:** Higher efficiency means less stored energy is lost; top 2026 batteries range from about **90% to 98%** [2].
4. **Warranty:** Many home batteries carry 10–15 year warranties, often guaranteeing around **70% capacity** at the end of the warranty period [2][3].
5. **System compatibility:** Some batteries are best paired with specific inverter ecosystems, such as Enphase or SolarEdge [2].
6. **Your utility rates:** Batteries are most financially attractive where net metering is weak, outages are frequent, or time-of-use electricity rates are high [2][3].
