In the UK, a typical home battery storage system costs around **£4,000–£6,500 installed** for a **5–10 kWh** system, which is a common size for an average home [1].

As a rough guide:

- **Small system, 3–5 kWh:** **£2,500–£4,000 installed** [1]
- **Typical system, 5–10 kWh:** **£4,000–£6,500 installed** [1]
- **Large system, 10–15 kWh:** **£6,000–£8,000 installed** [1]

The Energy Saving Trust gives a similar benchmark, saying battery storage can range from **£1,500 to £10,000**, with a **5 kWh battery system around £4,600** [4]. Which? also notes battery storage costs can range from **under £2,000 up to £10,000**, depending on capacity, type, brand and lifespan [3].

So, for an “average” UK home, expect roughly **£4,500–£6,000 installed**, depending on size and whether you need a new inverter or other electrical work.
