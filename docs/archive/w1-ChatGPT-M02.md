## What to prioritize when choosing

1. **Battery chemistry:** For most homes, **LFP / LiFePO4** is preferred because it is safer and long-lasting; several guides identify LFP as the leading chemistry for residential storage [4].
2. **Capacity, kWh:** This is how much energy the battery stores. Around 10–16 kWh suits many homes, while larger systems are better for whole-home backup or high evening use.
3. **Power, kW:** This determines how many appliances can run at once. High-capacity batteries with low power output can still struggle with large loads.
4. **Warranty:** Many batteries offer 10 years, while Enphase and some FranklinWH models offer longer warranties [2][3].
5. **Inverter compatibility:** If you already have solar, AC-coupled batteries such as Tesla, FranklinWH, or Enphase can be easier to retrofit; DC-coupled batteries often work best when paired with the same brand’s hybrid inverter [5].
6. **Installer support:** A great battery poorly installed is a bad system. Local installer experience and warranty support matter as much as the brand.
