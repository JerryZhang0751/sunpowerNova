For a typical detached house, you’ll usually need **10–20 solar panels**, but the exact number depends mainly on your electricity use, roof space, location, and panel wattage.

### Quick estimate

A common modern solar panel is around **400–450 watts**.

| Annual electricity use | System size needed | Approx. panels |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 3,000 kWh/year | 3–4 kW | 7–10 panels |
| 5,000 kWh/year | 5–6 kW | 12–15 panels |
| 8,000 kWh/year | 8–10 kW | 18–25 panels |

### Simple formula

1. Find your annual electricity use in kWh from your bill.
2. Estimate system size:

**System size in kW = annual kWh ÷ local annual solar production per kW**

As a rough rule:
- Sunnier areas: **1 kW of panels produces 1,300–1,700 kWh/year**
- Cloudier/northern areas: **1 kW produces 800–1,100 kWh/year**

Then:

**Number of panels = system size ÷ panel wattage**

Example:
If your house uses **5,000 kWh/year**, and 1 kW of solar produces **1,000 kWh/year**, you need a **5 kW system**.
With **420 W panels**:

**5,000 W ÷ 420 W = about 12 panels**

### Typical answer

**12–16 panels for a medium-sized home**
or
**4–7 kW of solar capacity**
