It depends on what you want the battery to do. A good home battery is sized by **energy capacity** in kWh and **power output** in kW.

## Quick rule of thumb

- **Essential backup only:** 5–10 kWh
  Fridge, lights, internet, outlets, maybe a sump pump.

- **Comfortable overnight backup:** 10–20 kWh
  Essentials plus some cooking, fans, TV, small appliances.

- **Whole-home backup:** 20–40+ kWh
  Especially if you want to run central A/C, electric heat, well pump, or large loads.

- **With solar for daily self-consumption:** often 10–20 kWh
  Enough to store excess daytime solar for evening/night use.

- **With electric heating, large A/C, or EV charging:** sizing can jump significantly.

## Simple sizing method

Estimate the loads you want to run during an outage:

| Load | Typical daily use |
|---|---:|
| Refrigerator | 1–2 kWh/day |
| Wi-Fi/router | 0.1–0.3 kWh/day |
| LED lights | 0.3–1 kWh/day |
| Laptop/phones | 0.2–0.8 kWh/day |
| TV | 0.3–1 kWh/day |
| Sump/well pump | varies widely |
| Central A/C | 10–30+ kWh/day |
| Electric heat | 30–100+ kWh/day |
| EV charging | 10–80+ kWh/session |

Formula:

**Battery size = daily backup energy × desired days of backup ÷ usable battery fraction**

Most batteries have about **85–95% usable capacity**, and you may want extra margin.

Example:
If your critical loads use **8 kWh/day** and you want **1 day** of backup:

**8 kWh ÷ 0.9 ≈ 9 kWh**

So you’d likely choose a **10 kWh battery**.

For 2 days:

**8 kWh/day × 2 ÷ 0.9 ≈ 18 kWh**

So you’d want around **20 kWh**.

## Don’t forget power output

Capacity is “how long it lasts.” Power output is “what it can run at once.”

You need enough inverter power for your largest simultaneous loads.

Examples:

- Essentials panel: **3–5 kW** may be enough.
- Whole-home with pumps/microwave/etc.: **7–12 kW**.
- Central A/C, electric dryer, range, or heat pump: may need **10–20+ kW** and surge capability.

## My practical recommendation

For many homes:

- **No solar, basic outage protection:** 10–15 kWh
- **Solar + backup for essentials:** 10–20 kWh
- **Solar + more whole-home backup:** 20–30 kWh
- **Want A/C or electric heat during outages:** size carefully, often 30+ kWh or load-manage those appliances.
