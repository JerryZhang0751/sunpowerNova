**Quick recommendations:**

- If you want the **most Powerwall-like whole-home backup**, look at **FranklinWH aPower**, **Generac PWRcell**, or **Panasonic EVERVOLT**.
- If you already have **Enphase solar**, the **Enphase IQ Battery 5P** is often the cleanest ecosystem fit.
- If you want **maximum scalability**, consider **HomeGrid Stack’d**, **GSL Energy**, or **Panasonic EVERVOLT**.
- If you want a **budget/off-grid option**, look at **EG4** or **Fortress**.
- If safety and cycle life matter, prioritize **LFP / LiFePO4 chemistry**, which is commonly highlighted for stability and longevity [2].

Before choosing, compare **usable kWh**, **continuous power output**, **surge power**, **whole-home vs critical-load backup**, **inverter compatibility**, **warranty**, **installer availability**, and **total installed cost**—not just the battery price.
