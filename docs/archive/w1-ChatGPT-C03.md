## Shortlist by use case

### 1. Best all-around for self-consumption: **Tesla Powerwall 3**
Choose this if you are installing a **new solar-plus-storage system** and want a simple, high-performance setup. Powerwall 3 combines a 13.5 kWh battery with an integrated hybrid inverter and supports both backup and time-of-use optimization [3]. It is especially strong where export compensation is low or peak rates are high.

### 2. Best for existing Enphase solar: **Enphase IQ Battery 5P**
Choose Enphase if your roof already uses Enphase microinverters or you want to add storage gradually. Each IQ 5P adds 5 kWh and 3.84 kW continuous power, so you can start small and expand later [1][2]. It is not always the cheapest per kWh, but the modularity and 15-year warranty are major advantages [1].

### 3. Best for larger homes: **FranklinWH aPower 2**
Choose FranklinWH if you have high evening consumption, want whole-home backup, or need strong load management. The aPower 2 offers 15 kWh usable capacity and high power output, and its aGate controller supports solar, grid, generator, and load-management functions [1][3].

### 4. Best if you already have SolarEdge: **SolarEdge Home Battery**
If your solar system already uses SolarEdge optimizers and a compatible Energy Hub inverter, staying inside the SolarEdge ecosystem can be the cleanest choice. Its 9.7 kWh battery is smaller than Tesla or FranklinWH, but integration and DC-coupled efficiency are its strengths [1][3].

### 5. Best modular DC-coupled international option: **BYD Battery-Box**
BYD Battery-Box systems are especially compelling in Europe and Asia-Pacific, where compatible hybrid inverters are common. They are modular, LFP-based, and well suited to self-consumption-focused homes that do not need an all-in-one U.S.-style battery system [1].

## My practical recommendation

- **New solar + storage:** Start with **Tesla Powerwall 3** or **FranklinWH aPower 2**.
- **Existing Enphase solar:** Choose **Enphase IQ Battery 5P**.
- **Existing SolarEdge solar:** Choose **SolarEdge Home Battery / BAT-10K**.
- **Large home / high evening loads:** Consider **FranklinWH aPower 2** or multiple **Powerwall 3** units.
- **Europe/APAC with hybrid inverter ecosystem:** Consider **BYD Battery-Box**.

For pure self-consumption, prioritize **usable kWh, round-trip efficiency, inverter compatibility, and smart time-of-use controls** over backup-only features. A typical home often needs roughly **10–15 kWh** for meaningful overnight solar shifting, while larger or highly electrified homes may need **20–30 kWh+** [1].
