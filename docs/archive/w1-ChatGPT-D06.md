A **10 kWh home battery can be enough for a family home**, but it depends on what you want it to do: **overnight solar use, backup during outages, or whole-home power**.

### Quick rule of thumb
A typical family home may use roughly **15–30 kWh of electricity per day**, though this varies a lot by country, home size, heating/cooling, and appliances.

A 10 kWh battery usually has about **9–10 kWh usable capacity**, depending on the model and settings.

### What 10 kWh can typically run
A 10 kWh battery could often cover:

- Fridge/freezer
- Lights
- Wi‑Fi/router
- TVs/computers
- Some cooking or microwave use
- Washing machine for a cycle
- Basic overnight household use

It may last:

| Usage level | Approx. duration from 10 kWh |
|---|---:|
| Essential loads only | 12–24+ hours |
| Normal evening/overnight use | 8–16 hours |
| Heavy use with AC, oven, dryer, EV charging | Much less |

### When 10 kWh is usually enough
A 10 kWh battery is often suitable if you want to:

- Store excess solar power for evening/night use
- Reduce grid imports during peak-price periods
- Keep essential appliances running during short outages
- Cover typical overnight electricity use

### When it may not be enough
You may need more than 10 kWh if you want to power:

- Electric heating
- Central air conditioning for many hours
- Electric hot water
- Tumble dryer
- Electric oven for long periods
- EV charging
- Whole-home backup for a full day or more

These loads can drain a 10 kWh battery quickly.

### Example
If your home uses **20 kWh per day**, a 10 kWh battery could cover about **half a day’s average consumption**. But if you only back up essential circuits using **400–800 watts**, it could last much longer.

### Bottom line
For many family homes, **10 kWh is a good mid-sized battery** and can be enough for overnight use or essential backup. But for **whole-house backup, heavy heating/cooling, or EV charging**, you may want **13–20+ kWh** or multiple batteries.

A good way to decide is to check your electricity bill:
**Daily usage in kWh ÷ desired backup hours = battery size estimate.**
