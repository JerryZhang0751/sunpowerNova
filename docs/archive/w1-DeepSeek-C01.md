## 💡 Key Considerations Before Buying

### Battery Chemistry Matters
**LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate)** dominates 2026 for good reason: 3,000–6,000 cycles, very low fire risk, and up to 100% depth of discharge. Older NMC batteries typically last only 1,500–3,000 cycles [1].

### Sizing: Match the Battery to Your Goal
- **Essential backup only** (fridge, lights, outlets): **10 kWh** is typically sufficient (~6–7 hours) [1]
- **Whole-home backup** (including HVAC): **20–30 kWh** needed, with costs pushing past $25,000 [1]
- **Time-of-use bill shifting**: **10–13.5 kWh** usually covers evening peak usage [1]

### Financial Reality Check
- In **California** (NEM 3.0, high peak rates ~$0.55/kWh): batteries can pay back in under 15 years [1]
- In **lower-rate states** (~$0.14/kWh): payback from bill savings alone can stretch to **25–34 years** — the value is primarily in backup/resilience [1]
- The **30% federal ITC** applies to standalone batteries if charged ≥70% from solar [1]

### Virtual Power Plant Income
VPP programs (available in select markets) can generate **$100–$300/year** in supplemental income by allowing your utility to dispatch your battery during grid stress events [1].
