In Europe, a **home solar PV system with a battery** typically costs about **€13,000–€40,000 installed**, depending on country, system size, roof complexity, labour costs, and battery capacity. A typical single-family home often uses **6–15 kWp of solar** and **5–15 kWh of battery storage**; larger homes with a heat pump or EV may use **10–20 kWh** of storage [2][3].

Typical installed price ranges for a PV system **with moderate battery storage of about 8–12 kWh** are:

| Region | Typical cost with battery |
|---|---:|
| **Northern Europe** — Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Norway | **€18,000–€28,000** |
| **Western Europe** — Germany, Austria, Belgium, Netherlands | **€16,000–€25,000** |
| **Southern Europe** — Spain, Portugal, Italy, Greece | **€14,000–€22,000** |
| **Eastern Europe** — Poland, Czechia, Romania, Hungary, Slovakia | **€13,000–€20,000** |

These ranges are for a typical residential solar installation with moderate storage; homes with an EV, heat pump, or both often cost more — roughly **€20,000–€40,000** depending on region and size [2].

If you already have solar panels and are adding only the battery, the extra cost is often roughly **€6,000–€12,000+** for a residential battery system, depending mainly on capacity, inverter compatibility, installation work, and whether backup power is included. For most European households, a battery size of **5–15 kWh** is common [3].

So, as a quick rule of thumb:
**Budget around €15,000–€25,000 for a normal family-home solar + battery system in much of Europe**, and more if you want a larger battery, EV charging, heat-pump coverage, or backup-power capability.
