There isn’t one “best” company for every home, but **Tesla is the best overall choice for many new solar + battery installations**, mainly because the **Powerwall 3** has very high power output, strong efficiency, an integrated inverter, and a polished app ecosystem. Reviews commonly rank it at or near the top for raw performance, with about **13.5 kWh capacity** and **~11.5 kW continuous power** depending on configuration/source [1][3].

That said, the best company depends on your situation:

- **Best overall / new solar install:** **Tesla — Powerwall 3**
  Best if you want high output for whole-home backup, strong efficiency, and simple app control [1][3].

- **Best for reliability, modular expansion, and warranty:** **Enphase — IQ Battery 5P**
  Best if you already have Enphase solar equipment or want to add storage in smaller 5 kWh increments; it also has a longer **15-year warranty** in several comparisons [1][2][3].

- **Best value / large-capacity backup:** **FranklinWH**
  Often cited as strong on cost per kWh, whole-home backup, and generator/retrofit flexibility, though its installer network may be smaller than Tesla’s or Enphase’s [2][3].

- **Best if you already have or want a standby generator:** **Generac — PWRcell**
  Generac is especially attractive when generator integration is a priority [4].

**My short answer:** If you’re starting from scratch, I’d look first at **Tesla Powerwall 3**. If you already have solar—especially Enphase microinverters—or care most about warranty and modularity, look at **Enphase**. Before choosing, get quotes from at least 2–3 local installers because installation quality, utility incentives, and compatibility can matter as much as the battery brand.
