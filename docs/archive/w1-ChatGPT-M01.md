### Key 2026 market context

Europe’s residential solar market is becoming less about “just panels” and more about **integrated home-energy systems**: rooftop PV, batteries, EV chargers, heat pumps, dynamic tariffs and energy-management software [3]. EUPD expects the European residential segment to add about **122 GW of new capacity over five years**, while residential storage adoption is also rising strongly [3].

If you mean **solar panel manufacturers used in European residential projects**, the leading global module brands for 2026 include **Jinko Solar, LONGi, Trina Solar, JA Solar, Tongwei, Canadian Solar, DMEGC, Astronergy, Risen Energy and Qcells** [2]. But for homeowners, the more relevant “top companies” are usually the **installer / energy-platform providers** listed above.
