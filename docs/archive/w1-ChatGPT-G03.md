### Battery size guide for an Italian home

- **Small home, 3–4 kWp PV:** 5 kWh battery
- **Typical family home, 4.5–6 kWp PV:** 7–10 kWh battery
- **Heat pump, induction cooking, or EV:** 10–15 kWh
- **Large home / high evening use:** 15–20 kWh

For most Italian residential systems, **around 7–10 kWh** is the practical sweet spot: big enough to shift daytime solar into evening use, but not so large that payback becomes poor.

### What to check before buying in Italy

1. **Inverter compatibility** — this is the biggest hidden issue; Huawei and SolarEdge batteries are ecosystem-locked, while BYD and Pylontech work with many hybrid inverters [4].
2. **LFP chemistry** — prefer LFP/LiFePO₄ for safety, long cycle life, and durability; modern residential batteries have largely moved to LFP [2][3][4].
3. **Backup function** — not every battery powers the house during a blackout; you may need a backup box/transfer switch.
4. **CEI 0-21 compliance** — make sure the inverter/battery setup is approved for Italian grid connection.
5. **Installer support in your region** — a good local installer matters as much as the battery brand.

**Bottom line:** For Italy, I’d shortlist **BYD Battery-Box Premium** as the best all-round choice, **Huawei LUNA2000** if you already use Huawei, and **Tesla Powerwall 3** if you want a premium all-in-one backup system.
