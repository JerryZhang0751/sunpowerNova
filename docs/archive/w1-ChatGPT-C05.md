A key point: **efficiency is not the only thing that matters**. High-efficiency panels often cost more, so you should compare **cost per watt, warranty, degradation rate, temperature coefficient, installer quality, and roof layout** [2][4]. For hot climates, look closely at the **temperature coefficient**, because solar panel output drops as panels get hotter [4].
