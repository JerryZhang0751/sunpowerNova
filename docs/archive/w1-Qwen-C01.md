## **Key Considerations for 2026**

**Battery Chemistry:** Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) batteries dominate the market due to longer cycle life (3,000-6,000 cycles vs. 1,500-3,000 for NMC), better safety, and deeper discharge capability [1].

**Federal Tax Credit:** The 30% Investment Tax Credit applies to standalone battery storage in 2026, provided the battery is charged at least 70% from solar energy [1].

**Cost Reality:** A typical 10-13.5 kWh battery system costs $10,000-$15,000 installed before incentives. After the 30% tax credit, expect to pay $7,000-$10,500 [1].

**Financial Payback:** Battery storage makes strong financial sense in states with high peak electricity rates (like California), reduced net metering, or frequent outages. In states with low rates and full retail net metering, payback can exceed 20 years [1][3].

**Best Combinations:**
- **For maximum efficiency:** Canadian Solar panels + Tesla Powerwall 3
- **For best value:** CertainTeed panels + MidNite Solar Powerflo 16
- **For whole-home backup:** High-efficiency panels + FranklinWH aPower 2 (stackable system)

The "best" system depends on your specific needs, roof space, local utility rates, and whether your primary goal is backup power, bill reduction, or both [1][3].
