For a typical single-family house in Germany, a rooftop solar PV system **plus a home battery** usually costs about:

| Household situation | Typical system size | Battery size | Installed cost in Germany / Western Europe |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Normal household, no EV/heat pump | 8–12 kWp | 8–12 kWh | **€16,000–€25,000** |
| With electric car | 10–14 kWp | 10–15 kWh | **€18,000–€28,000** |
| With heat pump | 10–15 kWp | 10–15 kWh | **€19,000–€29,000** |
| Heat pump + EV | 12–18 kWp | 15–20 kWh | **€24,000–€35,000** |

These ranges are for Western Europe including Germany and depend on roof complexity, installer pricing, battery brand, inverter, and electrical work [1].

As a rough split: a **6–10 kWp solar system without battery** is often around **€9,000–€15,000**, while adding a moderate **8–12 kWh battery** brings the total to around **€16,000–€25,000** [1]. Most European households with rooftop solar typically need around **5–15 kWh** of usable battery capacity, depending on consumption and whether they have an EV or heat pump [3].

Germany also has a major cost advantage: since 2023, qualifying residential solar panels and battery storage systems are generally subject to **0% VAT**, reducing upfront cost compared with the former 19% VAT rate [3].

**Rule of thumb:** for a standard German family house, budget **around €18,000–€25,000** for a good PV + battery setup; for a larger house with heat pump or EV, budget **€25,000–€35,000**.
